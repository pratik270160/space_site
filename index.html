// Case files

It has happened before.

SEP 1859 · THE CARRINGTON EVENT

The telegraph catches fire

Richard Carrington watched a flare burn across the Sun. Roughly 17 hours later, telegraph lines sparked, operators were shocked, and auroras glowed as far south as the Caribbean.

MAR 1989 · QUEBEC

A province goes dark in 90 seconds

A geomagnetic storm collapsed Hydro-Québec's grid. About six million people lost power for roughly nine hours.

FEB 2022 · STARLINK

Forty satellites never leave

SpaceX launched 49 Starlink satellites into a low parking orbit. A storm the next day swelled the upper atmosphere, the extra drag pulled them down, and up to 40 were lost.

MAY 2024 · THE GANNON STORM

Auroras at the equator's doorstep

The strongest geomagnetic storm in over two decades. Auroras appeared far south of their usual range, and farmers reported GPS guidance trouble on tractors in the middle of planting season.
